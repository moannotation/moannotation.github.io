FAQ

Questions companies ask

You're a new company. Why should we trust you with our data?

You shouldn't, on faith. That's why every engagement starts with a paid pilot on a scoped sample, measured against an accuracy threshold we agree with you in advance, with a full quality report on delivery. You risk one small batch, not your pipeline. We also sign a mutual NDA before any data moves, and our founder has worked hands-on inside the AI annotation industry across multiple training platforms.

What does a project cost?

It depends on task complexity, volume, turnaround and security requirements — which is why we ask for a data sample before quoting. We won't give you a number over a description, because a number given blind is a number we'd have to revise. Send a sample and you'll have a written quote with a committed accuracy threshold.

Do we have to use your tools?

No. If you have your own annotation or evaluation platform, our team works inside it — that's usually the cleanest option for your security team. Where you don't, we supply and configure the tooling (Label Studio, CVAT, Argilla, or a controlled spreadsheet for small text pilots).

How do you actually measure quality?

Four mechanisms running in parallel. Gold tasks: known-answer items seeded invisibly into each contributor's queue for an objective accuracy score. Random sampling: independent review of 20% of a new contributor's output, dropping to 5% once proven. Overlap: the same items assigned to multiple people to measure agreement. Error taxonomy: every error categorised, so we can tell an individual accuracy problem from an ambiguous-guidelines problem. You receive the numbers, not just the output.

How fast can you start, and how quickly can you scale?

A pilot typically starts within days of guidelines being agreed. Scaling is deliberate: we add contributors who have passed assessment and calibration on your specific guidelines, not whoever is available. We would rather tell you a realistic ramp than miss a deadline.

Can you handle sensitive or regulated data?

Tell us the requirements before the pilot and we'll scope to them — restricted access, work performed only in your environment, specific retention and deletion terms, and a DPA. Where a requirement is beyond what we can currently meet, we'll say so plainly rather than discover it mid-project.

Who exactly does the work?

Named, identity-verified individuals who have passed a skills assessment in the relevant area, been briefed on your project guidelines, and signed an individual NDA. Not an anonymous crowd. We can tell you who worked on your project and what they scored.
