How to join

The signup process, step by step

From application to your first paid task is typically 7–14 days, depending on how quickly you complete each stage and whether a matching project is open. Nothing here costs you money at any point.

1

Submit your application

5 minutes

Fill in the application form on this site. It opens a WhatsApp chat with your details already written out, so our onboarding team receives a complete application rather than a blank "hello".

  • Full name, email, and city
  • Your strongest skill area
  • Any relevant experience (optional)
  • The device and internet you'll be working with
Have ready: a working email address you check regularly.
2

Eligibility screening

Within 48 hours

We review your application against the basic requirements and reply either way. If we can't take you on right now, we'll tell you plainly and say whether it's worth reapplying later.

  • 18 years or older
  • Access to a laptop or desktop computer
  • Internet stable enough for a full working session
  • Strong written English (other languages are a bonus)
Outcome: invitation to identity verification, or an honest no.
3

Identity verification

1–2 days

Our clients trust us because we know exactly who does their work. This step is what makes that true — and it's why our contributors get access to projects that anonymous freelance platforms never see.

  • A valid government-issued ID (NIN slip, driver's licence, voter's card or passport)
  • A clear selfie for matching against the ID
  • Confirmation of your full legal name and date of birth
We never ask for: your BVN, bank password, card PIN, or any payment from you.
4

Skills assessment

45–90 minutes

A practical test in your chosen area — not a quiz. You'll do a small set of real-style tasks and we score them against a known answer key. This sets your starting level and the projects you can be matched to.

  • Text, image/video, evaluation, or coding track depending on your application
  • Includes deliberate edge cases — flagging uncertainty scores better than guessing
  • You may retake once after a waiting period if you don't pass first time
Tip: read the instructions twice. Most failures are instruction-reading failures, not skill failures.
5

Training & certification

2–4 hours, free

Free MO AI training covering how professional annotation actually works. Complete it and you hold an MO AI certification that stays with you across every project you take with us.

  • Annotation and evaluation standards
  • How our four-layer quality assurance works and what's expected of you
  • Confidentiality, data handling, and client IP obligations
  • How to read guidelines and when to flag instead of guess
Cost to you: nothing. Training is never charged for.
6

Sign your agreements

15 minutes

Before you touch client data, we put the relationship in writing — protecting the client's information and your position equally.

  • Non-disclosure agreement covering all project data
  • Contributor agreement setting out conduct, quality standards and payment terms
  • Your payout details recorded (account name and number only)
Read before signing. Ask us anything you're unsure about — a good question is never a problem.
7

Project matching & calibration

When a project opens

You join the verified pool. When a project fits your skills and languages, you're invited to it — starting with a calibration round where the whole team labels the same items and reviews disagreements together.

  • You see the exact pay rate in writing before you accept anything
  • Project guidelines and a live walkthrough are provided
  • Calibration confirms you're aligned before live work begins
Honest note: matching depends on live client demand. We won't promise work we don't have.
8

Work, review, get paid

Ongoing

You work through your assigned batches. Your output is reviewed — heavily at first, then lightly once you've proven accuracy — and you're paid on the terms in your project agreement.

  • 20% of your work sampled while new, dropping to 5% once proven
  • Feedback comes with the reason, so you can actually improve
  • Consistent accuracy earns priority access to higher-paying projects
Progression: proven contributors move into RLHF and writing work — our highest rate tier.

FAQ

Questions contributors ask

Is it free to join?

Yes, completely. We never charge you to register, train, get certified or work. Anyone asking you to pay a fee to join MO AI is not us — report it to us immediately.

How much will I earn?

It depends on the project, the work type and your certification level. RLHF and writing work pays the highest rates; entry-level annotation pays less while you build a track record. What we commit to: you see the exact rate in writing before you accept a task, never after. If we can't tell you the rate up front, don't take the work.

When and how do I get paid?

Payment terms are stated in your project agreement before you start, and paid to your bank account or preferred payout method. Payment is tied to accepted work — work that fails quality review may need correction before it's approved, which is why the training and guidelines matter.

Do I need experience?

Not for entry-level projects. Our training covers annotation standards, quality expectations and confidentiality. What we can't train is attention to detail and the willingness to flag an item you're unsure about instead of guessing. Those two habits are what separate contributors who last from those who don't.

Can I work from my phone?

Most projects need a laptop or desktop — annotation tools are demanding and accuracy suffers on a small screen. A few evaluation projects can work on tablet. If you don't have a computer yet, apply anyway and we'll tell you honestly whether anything currently fits.

Is this full-time? Can I do it alongside another job?

Most contributors work flexibly around other commitments. Projects have deadlines and quality standards, so reliability matters — but hours are generally yours to arrange within the project window.

How long does signup take from start to first task?

Typically 7–14 days: application (5 minutes), screening reply within 48 hours, identity verification (1–2 days), skills assessment (45–90 minutes), free training (2–4 hours), then agreements and project matching. The variable is project availability — we won't invent work that doesn't exist just to fill the pipeline.

What if I fail the skills assessment?

You can retake it once after a waiting period. Most first-time failures come from not reading the instructions carefully rather than lack of ability, so review the guidelines properly before your second attempt. If you don't pass the second time we'll tell you honestly and suggest which track might suit you better.

How do I know this isn't a scam?

Fair question, and you should ask it of everyone. We're a registered Nigerian company with a named founder, a public website, and a physical base in Abuja. We never ask you for money, and we never ask for your bank password or BVN — only the account details needed to pay you. Every legitimate communication from us comes from this website, our official WhatsApp number, or hello@moannotation.com.
